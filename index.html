Raymond (Ray) Joseph

Lead Applied Scientist at Salesforce

LLM safety · prompt-injection detection · toxicity detection · ethical-AI controls

I'm a machine-learning engineer working on AI safety: LLM guardrails, evaluation frameworks, and red teaming. Over the past decade I've built and deployed ML systems across finance, financial regulation, an early-stage startup, and high-assurance government environments, with a recent focus on making large language models safer and more reliable in production.

I'm currently a Staff Software Engineer at LegionIntel, where I work on retrieval-augmented generation and LLM safety systems. Before that I founded Modguard.ai, an AI-guardrails startup. Earlier I led ML-platform and model-governance work at FINRA and spent six years in quantitative analytics at Barclays in London.

My interests sit at the intersection of adversarial robustness, alignment, and evaluation: how we measure whether a model is safe, and how we build systems that stay reliable under adversarial pressure.
